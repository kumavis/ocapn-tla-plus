Counterexample trace from TLC for MC_NaivePromiseResolution
(NaivePromiseResolution routing on PromiseResolution).

This is a violating run of EndToEndRefFIFO: for the head sender on ref 1,
op:deliver-only sequence numbers must increase in the observable delivery log.

Chain model (ChainLength = 2): promise at position 1 resolves to terminal at
position 2. The MC pins a readable witness:

  HeadPeer = vatA          (only vatA issues PeerSend in this MC)
  host[1] = vatB           (resolver of p1 lives on vatB)
  host[2] = vatA           (terminal hop is local to vatA)

Shape of the bug: vatA pipelines op:deliver-only messages on ref 1 toward
vatB while p1 is unresolved (queued at vatB's LocalPromise(1).queue). vatB
resolves p1 and sends op:resolve to vatA. After vatA installs
RemotePromise(1).localResolution = ResRef(vatA, 2), naive routing lets
later sends take the post-resolution path and deliver directly to the
LocalTarget at vatA, while an earlier seq is still queued or on the wire,
so delivered order for (sender=vatA, ref=1) is not strictly increasing.

Exact delivered tail depends on TLC's shortest counterexample; one common
pattern is <<..., seq=2, ...>>, <<..., seq=1, ...>> or <<..., seq=3, ...>>,
<<..., seq=1, ...>> — in all cases a higher seq reaches the terminal before a
lower seq from the same sender on the same ref.

TLC breadth-first order is not wall-clock order: the debug log may schedule
ResolverResolve before the first PeerSend, or interleave steps differently on
each run. The mermaid trace (.tlc-logs/MC_NaivePromiseResolution.trace.md)
puts lastAction notes before channel diffs and labels logical client→resolver
intent (Note over vatA,vatB) even when the soundness fix routes some forwards
on the physical edge vatB->>vatA (resolver→client FIFO) instead of vatA->>vatB.
